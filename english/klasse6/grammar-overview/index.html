ENGLISH TENSES

Grammar Overview  ·  Klasse 6g

Gelb = wichtige Verbform

SIMPLE PRESENT

Gewohnheiten · regelmäßig

+ Aussage

I play football.

− Verneinung

I don’t play football.

? Frage

Do I play football?

+ We

We play football.

− We

We don’t play football.

? We

Do we play football?

SIGNALWÖRTER

every day
usually · always
never · sometimes

MERKE!

he / she / it → -s!
Nach does / doesn’t:
Grundform ohne -s!

PRESENT PROGRESSIVE

passiert gerade jetzt

+ Aussage

I am playing football.

− Verneinung

I am not playing football.

? Frage

Am I playing football?

SIGNALWÖRTER

now · at the moment
Look! · Listen!

MERKE!

am / is / are + -ing
I am · you/we/they are
he/she/it is

SIMPLE PAST

Vergangenheit · abgeschlossen

+ Aussage

I played football.

− Verneinung

I didn’t play football.

? Frage

Did you play football?

SIGNALWÖRTER

yesterday · ago
last Sunday · last week

MERKE!

Regelmäßig: play → played
Unregelmäßig: go → went
Nach did: Grundform!

PAST PROGRESSIVE

war gerade im Verlauf

+ Aussage

I was playing football.

− Verneinung

I wasn’t playing football.

? Frage

Was I playing football?

+ Were

We were playing football.

− Were

We weren’t playing football.

? Were

Were we playing football?

SIGNALWÖRTER

when · while

MERKE!

was / were + -ing
I/he/she/it was
you/we/they were

DIE HILFSVERBEN AUF EINEN BLICK

Present Progressive:   I am  ·  you/we/they are  ·  he/she/it is

Past Progressive:   I/he/she/it was  ·  you/we/they were
