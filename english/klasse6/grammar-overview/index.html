Die vier wichtigsten Zeitformen

Liebe Klasse 6g, hier seht ihr vier englische Zeiten im Vergleich. Alle Beispiele verwenden play (spielen). Vergleicht besonders die Verneinungen und Fragen. Die Signalwörter helfen euch, die passende Zeitform zu erkennen.

Simple Present

Was regelmäßig oder immer wieder passiert.

+ Aussage
I play football.
He plays football.
− Verneinung
I don't play football.
He doesn't play football.
? Frage
Do you play football?
Does he play football?
Signalwörter

every day · usually · always · never · sometimes

Merke!He, she, it – das -s muss mit! Aber nach does / doesn't steht play ohne -s.

Present Progressive

Was gerade jetzt passiert.

+ Aussage
I am playing football.
− Verneinung
I am not playing football.
? Frage
Am I playing football?
Signalwörter

now · Look! · Listen! · at the moment

Merke!am / is / are + Verb-ing
I am
you / we / they are
he / she / it is

Simple Past

Was in der Vergangenheit passiert ist.

+ Aussage
I played football.
− Verneinung
I didn't play football.
? Frage
Did you play football?
Signalwörter

yesterday · last Sunday · last week · two days ago · in 2020

Merke!Regelmäßig: play → played. Unregelmäßige Verben muss man lernen, z. B. go → went, see → saw. Nach did / didn't steht die Grundform.

Past Progressive

Was zu einem Zeitpunkt in der Vergangenheit gerade im Verlauf war.

+ Aussage
I was playing football.
− Verneinung
I wasn't playing football.
? Frage
Was I playing football?
Wichtige Wörter

when · while

Merke!was / were + Verb-ing
I / he / she / it was
you / we / they were
when und while sind Hinweise, aber nicht in jedem Satz nötig.

Die Hilfsverben auf einen Blick

Present Progressive: I am playing · You are playing · He / She / It is playing · We / You / They are playing

Past Progressive: I was playing · You were playing · He / She / It was playing · We / You / They were playing

Achtet auf diese typischen Fehler!

✅ He plays. → ✅ Does he play? (nicht: Does he plays?)

✅ She went. → ✅ Did she go? (nicht: Did she went?)

✅ They are playing. (nicht: They is playing.)

✅ We were playing. (nicht: We was playing.)
